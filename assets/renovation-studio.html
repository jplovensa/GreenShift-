How the estimate works

Only placed EPS partitions and checked rear/left retained-wall linings are included. Net EPS area × core thickness. Rear/left lining uses a butt joint so its corner is counted once. Opening geometry is deducted. Retained-wall opening areas are shown as centred rectangular cutouts. Supply volume adds the selected waste allowance. EPS core only: no skins, finishes, adhesives, framing or structural design. Confirm specifications and quantities in a measured design review.
